📊 Flipkart Sales Descriptive Analytics

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on Descriptive Data Analytics of Flipkart Product Sales Performance for the period January–June 2026. The objective is to analyze sales transactions, identify business trends, evaluate product performance, and generate actionable insights using data analysis and visualization techniques.

The analysis covers product sales, regional demand, revenue distribution, customer behavior, payment methods, seller performance, order status, and discount patterns.

🎯 Objectives

- Identify top-selling products based on quantity.
- Analyze state-wise and city-wise sales performance.
- Evaluate category-wise revenue and quantity sold.
- Understand monthly sales trends and seasonal patterns.
- Analyze order status, cancellations, and returns.
- Study customer types and payment method preferences.
- Compare seller performance and product discounts.
- Generate insights to support business decision-making.

📈 Key Business Insights

1. Product Performance

- Redmi Note 13 5G ranked first, with 233 units sold.
- boAt Rockerz 550 ranked second, with 215 units sold.
- Together, these two products accounted for approximately 48.5% of recorded unit sales.

2. Regional Sales Analysis

- Maharashtra was the leading state, generating approximately ₹13.21 lakh in sales.
- Karnataka ranked second, followed by Uttar Pradesh.
- The top three states contributed approximately 47.5% of total recorded sales.

3. Category-Wise Revenue

- Mobiles & Electronics generated approximately ₹47.69 lakh, contributing around 80.9% of total recorded sales.
- Large Appliances contributed approximately ₹4.41 lakh.
- Beauty & Personal Care recorded the lowest category revenue.

4. Monthly Sales Trend

- May 2026 was the highest-performing month, with sales of approximately ₹15.63 lakh.
- February recorded the lowest monthly sales, at approximately ₹6.04 lakh.
- Sales increased by approximately 104% from April to May.

5. Order Status Analysis

- Delivered: 666 orders (68.0%)
- In Transit: 138 orders (14.1%)
- Cancelled: 101 orders (10.3%)
- Returned: 75 orders (7.7%)

The combined cancellation and return rate is approximately 18%, highlighting an opportunity to investigate order fulfilment and customer satisfaction.

6. Customer & Payment Analysis

- UPI was the most common payment method, accounting for 40.0% of recorded transactions.
- First-time buyers represented 42.3% of orders.
- Repeat buyers represented 38.2%.
- Flipkart Plus members represented 19.5%.

💡 Business Recommendations

- Improve inventory planning for high-volume products.
- Prioritize logistics and fulfilment capacity in high-revenue regions.
- Investigate the causes of order cancellations and product returns.
- Develop customer retention strategies to increase repeat purchases.
- Evaluate discount effectiveness across products and customer segments.
- Use historical sales data to develop demand forecasting models.
- Identify growth opportunities in regions with lower recorded sales.
